Laser Trepanning & Ablation Technical Guide

1. Overview & Dimensions Guide

This client-side engineering calculator models diffraction-limited Gaussian beam propagation, laser-material ablation boundaries, and 3D precession trepanning kinematics across all 9 reference workbook sheets inside an AssemblyScript WebAssembly / Web Worker physics engine.

2. Coordinate Systems & Units

  • Scanlab PrecSYS: Uses diameter (µm) for XY positioning, true angle of incidence AOI in degrees (°), and Z focus position in µm (negative values shift focus toward workpiece).
  • Arges Elephant v2: Uses radius (µm) for XY positioning, precession amplitude as a percentage (%) of maximum attack angle, and Z focus as a percentage (%) of maximum translation.
  • Round Holes: Circular trepanning orbit with continuous angle of incidence.
  • Square Holes (Polyline): Supported on Scanlab scanheads; scales AOI across corners/flats to preserve uniform corner geometry.

3. Governing Optical & Clipping Equations

4. State Transition Mode & Safety Heatmap

In State Transition mode, the engine sweeps all 101 interpolation points (0% to 100%) from Start State to End State and renders a live Safety Heatmap strip under the progress scrubber (Green = Safe, Amber = Wall Overcut, Red = Nozzle Clipping).
